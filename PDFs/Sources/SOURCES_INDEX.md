## 5. Usage During Thesis Writing

### When Writing Introduction:
1. Cite **McKinsey 2025** (`mckinsey2025agentic`) for industrial relevance.
2. Cite **5G Americas** (`5g_americas_ibn2025`) for network autonomy goals.
3. Cite **Munir et al.** (`munir2023neuro`) for Digital Twin context.

### When Writing Methods (The "Brain"):
1. Cite **Wu et al.** (`wu2025leveraging`) for Level 4 AN architecture.
2. Cite **Roy et al.** (`roy2024federated`) for Neuro-symbolic reasoning.
3. Use **Hitzler** (`hitzler2021neuro`) to define NeSy AI.

### When Writing Fault Diagnosis (The "Evidence"):
1. Cite **Fontaine et al.** (`fontaine2025single`) for bias in agent interaction.
2. Cite **Multi-Agent-as-Judge** (`maj_eval_2024`) for evaluation methodology.
